# Dragon Egg Energy Siphon

The **Dragon Egg Energy Siphon** is a **[passive generator](/docs/blocks/generators/passive_generators)** that produces energy from the <McItem slug="minecraft:dragon_egg" inline={true}/>.

| Tier   | Generation Rate | Output   | Storage |
|--------|------------------|----------|---------|
| Medium | 4 E/t            | 128 E/t  | 1 kE    |
